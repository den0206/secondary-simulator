Privacy Policy

Created and updated: October 15, 2026

The developer of tana (the “Provider”) handles information in the app and its public website as follows.

1. Processing on your Mac

tana scans Claude Code and Codex data folders that you select and authorize. It uses file names, paths, sizes, modification times, conversation titles and text, and related files to display storage use, preview conversations, review cleanup selections, and move and restore items from the Trash.

This processing happens on your Mac. tana does not send scanned files or conversation content to the Provider's servers, the agent providers, or AI services.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Folder access permissions (security-scoped bookmarks) and agent identifiers are kept in the app's storage so it can access your selected folders again.
  • Cleanup history stores dates, agents, categories, counts, sizes, and aggregate outcomes. It does not store file names, individual paths, or conversation text. It retains up to 100 entries and 256 KiB, removing older entries when a limit is reached.
  • Restore records store relative original paths, Trash paths, sizes, cleanup dates, and item and folder identifiers. They do not contain conversation text or copies of files. The restore screen uses records from the latest 20 cleanups within 30 days; older or excess records are excluded when records are loaded. You can clear saved records in the app.

Conversation previews and scan results are processed in memory. tana does not create a persistent cache of complete scan results or conversation text.

3. Revoking access and clearing records

Remove a folder in Folder Settings to revoke its saved access permission. Clear cleanup history in Cleanup History and restore records in Recently moved. Clearing either type of record does not delete the agent's original data or items in the Trash. Clearing restore records prevents tana from restoring those items.

Files moved to the Trash remain there until macOS or the user deletes them. The 30-day limit for restore records does not guarantee that files will stay in the Trash for 30 days. Information included in backups is subject to the backup settings.

4. Collection, advertising, and tracking

tana has no account registration, advertising, analytics, usage tracking, or custom crash-reporting system. The Provider does not collect your files or usage information from the app. Diagnostics provided by macOS or the App Store are subject to Apple's settings and policies.

5. Website and support requests

The public website is served through Google's Firebase Hosting. No analytics, advertising SDKs, or custom cookies have been added to the tana pages. Google may process access information such as IP addresses to deliver the pages. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Email addresses and messages received through support requests are used to respond and investigate issues. They are kept for as long as needed to handle the request and deleted when no longer needed. Email service providers also take part in delivering messages. Do not send conversation files or credentials.

6. Requests and changes

For questions or deletion requests, email mac.pop@icloud.com. App data is stored on your Mac, so the Provider cannot delete it remotely. Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
