Help & support

See what takes up space

View storage by agent and category, and explore its daily distribution. Preview conversations and related file details before choosing what to clean up. When limits or errors leave a scan incomplete, tana shows the scope of the results.

Review before moving to the Trash

Check the selected items before moving them. In-use items and protected settings, credentials, and databases are excluded. Conversations and unverified items require review. Deleting a conversation may prevent you from resuming it in the agent.

Put recently moved items back

Restore items to their original location while a restore record exists and the items remain in the Trash. Records cover the latest 20 cleanups within 30 days. Existing files are never overwritten. Items cannot be restored after the Trash is emptied.

Processed on your Mac

You choose which agent folders tana can access. File scanning, conversation previews, and cleanup happen on your Mac. Conversations and files are not sent to the developer or AI services.

Sizes shown are logical file sizes. Moving items to the Trash does not necessarily increase free disk space by that amount. Back up important data before cleanup.
