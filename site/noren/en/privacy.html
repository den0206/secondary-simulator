Privacy Policy

Effective: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how the developer of Noren (“we”) handles information in the app.

1. What we don’t collect

Noren has no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We never receive information about you.

2. Screen Time information

To show the cover, Noren uses Apple’s Screen Time API (Family Controls) with your permission, for your own device only. The apps you choose as “Visible Apps” are stored on your device as tokens issued by Apple. Noren cannot read app names or usage time from these tokens, and does not send them off your device.

3. Location (only if you register your home)

Noren asks for location access only when you register your home in “Off at Home”. If you don’t register it, Noren doesn’t use your location.

Your home location is handed to the iOS location monitoring feature and used only to detect when you arrive or leave. Noren doesn’t store the coordinates. To show an approximate place name (city and area) while your home is registered, Noren sends the coordinates to Apple’s map service once when you register. The place name text is stored on your device. Noren doesn’t keep a history of when you leave or arrive.

You can delete your home location with “Delete Home Location” in the app. You can turn off location access in iPhone Settings → Apps → Noren → Location.

4. Information stored on your device

Noren stores your settings, such as the on/off state, Visible Apps and cover style, on your device. Deleting the app deletes this information.

5. This website and contacting us

This website is served by Google Firebase Hosting, and Google may process access information needed to serve it. We don’t use analytics, ads or our own cookies. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and look into the issue, and delete them when no longer needed.

6. Questions and changes

For questions about your information, email mac.pop@icloud.com. If we change this policy, we will post the update on this page.
