Support

Contact

Email mac.pop@icloud.com. Please include the app version, your device model, iOS version, and what you did when the problem happened.

The cover doesn’t appear

Check that Noren isn’t off on its Home screen. The cover appears on the first app you open after locking your phone for a minute or more. To cover apps right away, touch and hold the Lock Screen button to turn it off, then again to turn it on. Apps you chose as Visible Apps never get a cover.

The Lock Screen button doesn’t respond or doesn’t change

iOS requires you to touch and hold Lock Screen buttons, so they aren’t pressed by accident. If the button doesn’t change after an app update, remove it in Lock Screen customization and add “Noren ON/OFF” again.

Notification content appears on the Lock Screen

Noren can’t hide Lock Screen notifications. In iPhone Settings → Notifications → Show Previews, choose “When Unlocked”.

Noren doesn’t turn off at home or back on when you leave

Check that location access is set to “Always”. iOS can take a few minutes to detect when you arrive or leave.

Stop using Noren

Delete the app to remove the cover and all settings. You can review Screen Time access in iPhone Settings → Screen Time.
